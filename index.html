Hi, I'm Zixuan Xu, a PhD researcher in the School of Electrical and Computer Engineering at The University of Sydney, supervised by Prof. Zihuai Lin and Prof. Marjorie Valix.

My work connects artificial intelligence with practical engineering problems. I explore retrieval-augmented generation, knowledge-guided medical AI and computer vision, alongside predictive modelling for water infrastructure.

My research interests include:

  • Large language models and retrieval-augmented generation
  • Clinical trial matching and knowledge graph reasoning
  • Computer vision and medical image understanding
  • Failure prediction and infrastructure condition assessment

Before my PhD, I worked as an AI Algorithm Engineer, developing medical question-answering and computer vision applications. I also teach practical labs in IoT wireless sensing and networking.

I'm interested in research collaboration and opportunities to bring AI into real-world applications. Let's connect.
