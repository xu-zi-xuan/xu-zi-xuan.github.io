## About me

I am a PhD researcher in the School of Electrical and Computer Engineering at **The University of Sydney**. My work connects artificial intelligence with applications in healthcare and infrastructure, spanning retrieval-augmented generation, knowledge-guided medical AI, computer vision and water asset deterioration modelling.

Before my PhD, I worked as an **AI Algorithm Engineer**, developing medical question-answering and vision-based rehabilitation assessment applications. Alongside my research, I teach practical labs in IoT wireless sensing and networking and mentor students through the Engineering Vacation Research Internship Program.

## Research interests

- **Language models & retrieval** — retrieval-augmented generation and multi-aspect evidence retrieval.
- **Medical AI & reasoning** — clinical trial matching, knowledge graphs and medical image understanding.
- **Computer vision** — medical segmentation and vision-based movement assessment.
- **Infrastructure intelligence** — failure prediction, concrete corrosion and asset condition assessment.

## Selected publications

### [MAQ-Retrieval: Multi-Aspect Queries Retrieval for Large Language Models](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/abstract/document/11397447)

**IEEE Transactions on Artificial Intelligence · 2026**

Weights different aspects of a query and uses convex-hull-based joint retrieval to capture connections between them and reduce noisy context in retrieval-augmented generation.

### [Just 32 Tokens: Medical Referring Image Segmentation via Next-Token Mask Prediction](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-032-38082-1_24)

**MICCAI 2026 · Lecture Notes in Computer Science**

Generates medical segmentation masks from text descriptions using just 32 discrete tokens, with mask supervision, clinical reasoning prompts and token-order prediction supporting spatial coherence.
